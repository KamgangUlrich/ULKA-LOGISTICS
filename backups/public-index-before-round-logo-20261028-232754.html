Calculation method

Volumetric weight per parcel = length × width × height ÷ 6000.
Total actual weight = actual weight per parcel × quantity.
Chargeable weight = higher of total actual and total volumetric weight.
Estimated cost = chargeable weight × shared air rate.

The rate is managed by an administrator in the dashboard. Carrier rounding, minimum charges, surcharges and other fees may apply. This is an estimate only.
